# Royal Crest data attribution

- Elevation: USGS 1 Meter 17 x42y458 OH_Statewide_Phase1_2019_B19. USGS 3D Elevation Program; public domain. Retrieved 2026-11-25; survey/product date: 2023-07-04.
- Golf features: OpenStreetMap contributors, © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under [ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Retrieved 2026-11-25.
- Scorecard (par, handicap and tee yardages): published scorecard at <https://stymie.golf/golf-courses/ohio/lorain-county/columbia-station/royal-crest-golf-club>. Retrieved 2026-11-25.

This course data is derived from these sources. Fairway, green and tee shapes, bunkers, water and cart paths come from OpenStreetMap; the surface bands (cuts, collars) and blue tee pads (white pad moved back along the first leg of the hole line by the scorecard yardage difference) are derived by `tools/course_import`.
